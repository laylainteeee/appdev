# Inuyasha



![Anime](https://img.shields.io/badge/Genre-Fantasy%20Adventure-red)



## About
**Inuyasha** is a Japanese anime and manga series by *Rumiko Takahashi*. It follows a modern-day girl who travels to Japan's feudal era.

## Main Characters
1. Inuyasha
2. Kagome Higurashi
3. Miroku
4. Sango
5. Shippo

## Why I Like It
- Great mix of action and romance
- Memorable characters
- Epic story about the **Shikon Jewel**

> "Feudal Fairy Tale" is how the series is often described.
